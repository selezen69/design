### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Grounded in the category and the actual psychology of hiring an interior designer, but not yet grounded in *this* studio's identity. The section order (Hero → Portfolio → About → Services → Process → Reviews → Contact) is the right sequence for a high-consideration, multi-month purchase. The visual language (dark Ken Burns hero, graphite/cream/gold palette, serif-italic accent word, tracked-uppercase micro-labels) is competent "quiet luxury interior-design portfolio" but a shared category convention, reinforced by placeholder identity data still live in `src/data/studio.js` (`studio.name = "Студия дизайна интерьеров"`, `telegram = "yourhandle"`).

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json src` on static source came back clean (0 findings, exit 0). The live browser-injected detector found what static scan couldn't:
- `clipped-overflow-container` on Hero — likely false positive (deliberately contains the Ken Burns background animation).
- `all-caps-body` fired twice — once on the Hero eyebrow, once on a data-driven portfolio subtitle (51 chars, only exists at runtime).
- `hero-eyebrow-chip` — the tracked-caps eyebrow pattern repeats in 7 files (About, Services, Portfolio, Process, Reviews, Contact, ProjectDetail); a deliberate identity motif, also evidence for the specificity verdict.

**Visual overlays:** Injection succeeded and the detector ran live in the page; tab and helper server have since been closed/stopped per cleanup requirement, no overlay open now.

### Overall Impression

Structural/system-level design work is good — heuristics 8 and 4 both score 4, section sequencing shows real thought about renovation-buyer psychology. Held back mostly by unfinished data and accessibility gaps, not taste: placeholder contact info live in production, text contrast failing WCAG AA on the line meant to reassure users about their data, form labels not programmatically linked to inputs. Biggest opportunity: empty Reviews section sits directly above the contact form.

### What's Working

1. Section sequencing mirrors real buyer psychology — photos first, then credibility, priced services, then a Process section with honest duration ranges.
2. Restrained, purposeful visual system — one accent color, disciplined type scale, consistent kickers; Services/Process reuse the same shape without feeling repetitive.
3. Honest conditional data rendering in `ProjectDetail` — missing fields are omitted rather than faked.

### Priority Issues

**[P1] Placeholder trust signals live in production, right above the contact form**
- Why it matters: `studio.telegram = "yourhandle"`, `studio.phone` placeholder, both `// TODO` but rendered as real clickable contact methods, right below an empty Reviews section.
- Fix: Replace `src/data/studio.js` placeholders with real values; replace apologetic empty-review state with a different trust signal.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Systematic low-contrast text, including the data-disclosure line**
- Why it matters: gold accent (`#c9a96e`) used for kickers measures 2.11–2.24:1 on white/cream (needs 4.5:1). Privacy-consent line under submit (`text-silver` on white) measures 1.74:1, references a policy with no link.
- Fix: Reserve gold accent for backgrounds/borders/numerals; darken for text use (~`#8a6a3a`). Switch consent line to `text-stone`; add real policy link.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] Contact-form inputs have no programmatic label association**
- Why it matters: all four `<label>` elements have `for={null}`, inputs `id=""` — the site's entire lead-capture mechanism. Phone field accepts any string.
- Fix: Add matching `id`/`htmlFor` pairs; add pattern/hint to phone field.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] `ProjectDetail` overlay has no standard exit paths**
- Why it matters: no keydown/Escape/history handling anywhere in `src/`. Only exit is the "Назад" button despite the overlay behaving like a real page.
- Fix: Add Escape listener calling onClose; push/pop history entry, or promote to a real route.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Mobile tap targets fall well under 44×44px**
- Why it matters: at 375px, filter buttons (~35×21px) and mobile nav links (327×20px) are roughly half the recommended height; the menu's own CTA is correctly sized (327×44px) in the same menu.
- Fix: Add vertical padding to filter buttons/mobile nav links to reach ~44px.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

### Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: empty-reviews line directly above the contact form; Telegram link points nowhere real; "от" pricing with no worked example or FAQ.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**: unlabeled form fields; kicker/price text at 2.1–2.24:1 contrast; all inputs strip native focus outline with only a weak border-color substitute; no Escape handler on ProjectDetail.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: ~20px tap targets on filters/mobile nav; form state not persisted across interruption/reload.

### Minor Observations

- Navbar has 6 top-level links, at the edge of the ≤5 grouping guideline.
- No active-section highlighting in Navbar while scrolling.
- "Лофт в саду" project's missing area/location/year renders as a nearly empty meta row with no framing.
- Hero's Ken Burns animation has no `prefers-reduced-motion` guard.
- `/showcase` internal QA harness ships in the same bundle as the public site with no visible auth/robots exclusion.
- Tracked-caps eyebrow treatment repeats in 7 files — deliberate motif, but also evidence the design reads as "premium interior-design category" rather than "this specific studio."

### Questions to Consider

1. If the real trust gap is 4 projects and zero reviews, what's the story to tell instead of apologizing for it?
2. Is "от 5 000 ₽/м²" without a worked total helping qualify serious clients, or adding friction?
3. Should `ProjectDetail` be a routed page instead of an overlay with no back-button support?
